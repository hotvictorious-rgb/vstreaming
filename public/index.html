Start the Hub

Double-click the "Victorious Streaming Hub" icon on your Desktop. The local server starts and opens this Director Command Center.

2

Connect Mobile Phones

Click "📲 Connect Phone" on any camera slot (Cam 1–6). Have your church volunteers scan the QR code using their phone's camera.

3

Add to OBS Studio

Click "📋 Copy OBS Link" on that camera. Inside OBS, click + → Browser Source, paste the link, and set width 1920 by height 1080.

4

Live Broadcast & Tally

Switch scenes in OBS or use the "🔴 CUT LIVE" buttons above. The phone screen pulses RED on air and GREEN on standby!
